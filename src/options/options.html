InkPage — remembered sites

Every site keeps its own switches. Remove a site to make it follow the defaults again.

How to use

  1. Open the page menu in Safari's address bar, tap InkPage, and switch it on for the site.
  2. Tap the right side of the screen for the next page, the left side to go back.
  3. Choose Book in the panel to read the article alone, laid out like an e-book; swipe or tap to turn pages.
  4. In Book, tap the top of the page for the toolbar; Aa changes the font, layout and paper.
  5. Refresh the screen in the panel clears ghosting on e-ink displays.
