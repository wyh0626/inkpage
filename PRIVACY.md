# Privacy Policy · 隐私政策

_Last updated · 最后更新: 2026-11-02_

## English

InkPage does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

- **What is stored**: your settings (which sites InkPage is on for, your display and typesetting choices, elements you chose to hide) and, in book view, the position you reached in each article. They are kept in your browser's extension storage on your device and never leave it.
- **Network**: InkPage makes no network requests of its own. It only loads files that ship inside the extension (its code and fonts).
- **Page content**: InkPage restyles the pages you turn it on for, inside your browser. It does not read your browsing out of the page or send it anywhere.
- **No accounts, analytics, advertising or remote code.**
- **Removing data**: uninstalling InkPage deletes all of its stored data. You can also reset everything from the extension's options page.

Questions: open an issue in the project's repository or contact the developer listed on the store page.

## 中文

墨页不收集、不上传、不出售、不分享任何个人数据。

- **会保存什么**：你的设置（在哪些网站开启、显示和排版选项、你选择隐藏的元素），以及书页模式下每篇文章读到的位置。这些只保存在你本机浏览器的扩展存储里，不会离开你的设备。
- **网络**：墨页自己不发起任何网络请求，只加载扩展包里自带的文件（代码和字体）。
- **网页内容**：墨页只在你的浏览器里，为你开启的网页调整样式，不会读取或发送你的浏览内容。
- **没有账号、统计分析、广告或远程代码。**
- **删除数据**：卸载墨页会删除它保存的全部数据；也可以在扩展的设置页里一键全部重置。

如有问题，请在项目仓库提 issue，或通过商店页面上的开发者联系方式联系。
